01 · AT THE DOOR

Getting in

How do I get in at 3am?+

Your booking confirmation email contains a door access link. It activates 10 minutes before your session starts and expires 10 minutes after it ends. Tap the link in the email, door unlocks, walk in.

Is anyone actually there?+

Nope. Par Out is unstaffed by design — that's how we keep the 24/7 hours viable. Cameras are active throughout the facility for everyone's safety.

What if my access link doesn't work?+

First, double-check that you're within your booking window — the link goes live 10 minutes before your start time, not earlier. If it still won't open, contact us and we'll sort it out as fast as we can.

02 · BEFORE YOU ARRIVE

Booking, rates & lessons

What's your cancellation policy?+

Cancel at least 2 hours before your start time and you're refunded or credited. Later than that and the session is forfeited.

How long should I book for 9 or 18 holes?+

Rough math: about 30 minutes per person for 9 holes, about an hour per person for 18. Book your block to match so you're not racing the clock on the back nine.

Can I host a group or party?+

Yes — book multiple bays at once for bigger groups. For full facility buyouts (private events, corporate outings, parties), email us and we'll set it up.

Do I need to be a member to play?+

No — anyone can book. Members save $10/hr on every session and get a 30-day advance booking window; the public gets 7 days. Full breakdown here.

Does my whole group get the member rate?+

The member rate applies to the bay, but the account holder must be in the bay during the session. Bring whoever — they play at your rate.

How do lessons work?+

Each coach sets their own price and availability. You book directly with them; they meet you in the bay. Par Out covers the bay and the tech; the coach covers the teaching. Coaches are being lined up now — see the lessons section.

03 · MID-SESSION

In the bay

Do I need to bring my own clubs?+

Yes. At this time we do not have loaner clubs. This is something we're actively looking into.

How many people fit in a bay?+

Up to 4 players on the simulator per session. The lounge area around each bay comfortably seats 4 — fine for a small group hanging out while a foursome plays. If you're planning a special event, reach out and we'll try to work with you.

Can I bring food?+

Yes. Par Out is bring-your-own-food. Order a pizza, pack a cooler, grab takeout on the way.

Alcohol?+

Non-alcoholic drinks only, please. We're not licensed to serve or allow alcohol on the premises.

Are kids allowed?+

Yes — anyone under 18 must be accompanied by a parent or guardian who's also in the bay. Great first-golf experience; no one's watching if their swing looks weird.

04 · WHEN IT BREAKS

When the tech acts up

Nobody's on site to reboot it for you, so here's exactly what we'd do if we were standing in the bay with you. Any of it not working? Call [PHONE].

The sim froze mid-round. How do I unstick it?+

Treat it like any frozen app. Hit the Windows key to bring up the taskbar, right-click the Trackman software, and close it. Re-open it with the orange TPS icon — it reboots clean and you're back on the tee.

How do I recalibrate?+

Open the menu and look top-right for the Calibrate button. Tap Auto-target and the sim dials itself in. No tools, no guesswork.

My session started but the projector's dark.+

Give us a call first. If the projector's blinking red, its power button sits on the underside — a gentle tap with a club brings it back.

The sim glitched mid-round — now what?+

We apologize for any technical issues. Most clear with a quick restart: Windows key, close the software on the taskbar, reload, log back in, pick up where you left off. Still stuck? Call us. We'll add time if the bay's free or move you to another bay. If neither is possible, we'll work with you to make it right.
