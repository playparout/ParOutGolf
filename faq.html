01 · AT THE DOOR

Getting in

How do I access the facility?+

Ten minutes before your session, we email you a door access link. It works from then until 10 minutes after your session ends. Tap the link, the door unlocks, walk in.

Is the facility staffed?+

Par Out is unstaffed by design. If you have issues, please call or email us. Cameras are active throughout the facility for everyone’s safety.

What if my access link doesn’t work?+

First, double-check that you’re within your booking window — the link goes live 10 minutes before your start time, not earlier. If it still won’t open, contact us and we’ll sort it out as fast as we can.

02 · BEFORE YOU ARRIVE

Booking, rates & lessons

What’s your cancellation policy?+

Cancel at least 2 hours before your start time and you’re refunded or credited. Later cancellations and no-shows aren’t refunded.

How long does 9 or 18 holes take?+

Plan for 30 minutes per person for 9 holes and 1 hour per person for 18 holes.

Can I host a group or party?+

Yes — book multiple bays at once for bigger groups. For full facility buyouts (private events, corporate outings, parties), email us and we’ll set it up.

Do I need to be a member to book?+

No — anyone can book. Members save $10/hr on every session and get a 30-day advance booking window; the public gets 7 days. Full breakdown here.

Does my whole group get the member rate?+

The member rate applies to the bay, but the account holder must be in the bay during the session. Bring whoever — they play at your rate.

How do lessons work?+

Each coach sets their own price and availability. You book through Alba Play; they meet you in the bay. Par Out covers the bay and the tech; the coach covers the teaching. Coaches are being lined up now — see the lessons section.

03 · MID-SESSION

In the bay

Do I need to bring my own clubs?+

Yes. At this time we don’t have loaner clubs. It’s something we’re actively looking into.

How many players fit in a bay?+

Up to 4 players per bay. Each bay has 4 seats. If you want to plan something bigger, please reach out and we will work with you.

Can I bring food and drinks?+

You’re welcome to bring in any outside food and non-alcoholic drinks. Please clean up at the end of your session to keep the space ready for the next group.

Alcohol?+

Non-alcoholic drinks only, please. We’re not licensed to serve or allow alcohol on the premises.

Are kids allowed?+

Yes — anyone under 18 must be accompanied by a parent or guardian who’s also in the bay. Great first-golf experience; no one’s watching if their swing looks weird.

04 · WHEN IT BREAKS

When the tech acts up

Nobody’s on site to reboot it for you, so here’s exactly what we’d do if we were standing in the bay with you. Any of it not working? Call (518) 727-3442.

The sim froze or glitched mid-round. What now?+

Treat it like any frozen app. Hit the Windows key to bring up the taskbar, right-click the Trackman software, and close it. Re-open it with the orange TPS icon, log back in, and pick up where you left off. Still stuck? Call us. We’ll add time if the bay’s free or move you to another bay. If neither is possible, we’ll work with you to make it right.

How do I recalibrate?+

Open the menu and look top-right for the Calibrate button. Tap Auto-target and the sim dials itself in. No tools, no guesswork.

My session started but the projector’s dark.+

Call us at (518) 727-3442 and we’ll get it back on for you.
