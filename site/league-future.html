How it works

Four steps to the tee.

Grab a partner

Register as a 2-person team and pick your division — competitive if you're chasing the cup, casual if you're here for the fun.

Play the weekly course

A new course is picked every week. Book a bay and play it any time from Monday to Sunday — whenever the two of you can line up a slot.

Submit your scramble score

Both of you tee off, you play the best ball, and repeat until it's holed. One attempt per week — the Trackman logs the score for you.

Climb the Order of Merit

Your weekly rank earns points. We count your best 10 of 12 weeks toward a 100-point Order of Merit, and the top teams play it out in a season-ending bracket.

Gameplay

The rules, in three.

The format

2-person scramble, stroke play, no handicap influence. Both partners hit, you pick the best ball, and you go again from there until the hole's done. Your hole score is the team's best work.

Scoring & Order of Merit

Each week you earn points based on your rank. We drop your two worst weeks, so your best 10 of 12 count toward a 100-point Order of Merit that sets the playoff field.

On the sim

Aimed putting with 8-foot gimmes. Yardages and pin difficulty are based on your division. One attempt per week, so make it count.

Good to know

League questions.

How do we submit our weekly score?
Play that week's course any time Monday through Sunday and the Trackman logs your scramble score automatically. One attempt per week, so book a time you can finish all 18.
How long does a round take?
Budget about 90 minutes for a 2-person scramble. Book a bay with enough runway to finish before your slot ends.
Can we miss a week?
Life happens. You can play the upcoming week's course early, or make up one missed week. Beyond that those weeks score zero — but we only count your best 10 of 12, so one slip won't sink you.
What if we tie for the best score?
Trackman breaks regular-season ties on back-nine performance. In the playoffs we run a tiebreaker round so there's always a clear winner.
Our division feels too tough — can we move?
Maybe. We set divisions by handicap, distance, and early results, and we'll move a team after week 1 if it's clearly a better fit — you'll get to make up that week. Heads up: we move teams up, too.
We're out of playoff range. Why keep playing?
Teams that play every week and miss the bracket get 50% off the next season — just email us so we can apply it.
The sim's acting up mid-round — what do we do?
Restart steps and full tech troubleshooting live on our FAQ page. Short version: call us and we'll add time, move you to another bay, or fix your scorecard so your round still counts.
