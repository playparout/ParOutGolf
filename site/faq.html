Getting in

How do I get in at 3 am?
Your booking confirmation email contains a door access link. It activates 10 minutes before your session starts and expires 10 minutes after it ends. Tap the link in the email, door unlocks, walk in.
Is anyone actually there?
Nope. Par Out is unstaffed by design — that's how we keep the 24/7 hours viable. Cameras are active throughout the facility for everyone's safety.
What if my code doesn't work?
First, double-check the code and make sure you're within your booking window. If it still won't open, text the number on your confirmation email and we'll sort it out fast.

Bays & booking

Do I need to bring my own clubs?
Yes. At this time we do not have loaner clubs. This is something we are actively looking into.
What's your cancellation policy?
Cancel at least 2 hours before your start time and you're refunded / credited. Later than that and the session is forfeited.
How many people fit in a bay?
Up to 4 players on the simulator per session. The lounge area around each bay comfortably seats 4 — fine for a small group hanging out while a foursome plays. If there is a special event, please reach out to us via the contact and we will try to work with you.
How long should I book for 9 or 18 holes?
Rough math: about 30 minutes per person for 9 holes, about an hour per person for 18. Book your block to match so you're not racing the clock on the back nine.

On the sim

The sim froze mid-round. How do I unstick it?
Treat it like any frozen app. Hit the Windows key to bring up the taskbar, right-click the Trackman software, and close it. Re-open it with the orange TPS icon — it reboots clean and you're back on the tee.
How do I recalibrate?
Open the menu and look top-right for the Calibrate button. Tap Auto-target and the sim dials itself in. No tools, no guesswork.
My session started but the projector's dark.
Give us a call first. If the projector's blinking red, its power button sits on the underside — a gentle tap with a club brings it back.
The sim glitched mid-round — now what?
We apologize for any technical issues. Most clear with a quick restart: Windows key, close the software on the taskbar, reload, log back in, pick up where you left off. Still stuck? Call us. We'll add time if the bay's free or move you to another bay. If both of those are not possible, we will work with you to make it right.

Food & drink

Can I bring food?
Yes. Par Out is bring-your-own-food. Order a pizza, pack a cooler, grab takeout on the way.
Alcohol?
Non-alcoholic drinks only, please. No BYOB. We're not licensed to serve or allow alcohol on the premises.

Who can play

Are kids allowed?
Yes — anyone under 18 must be accompanied by a parent or guardian who's also in the bay. Great first-golf experience; no one's watching if their swing looks weird.
Can I host a group or party?
Yes — book multiple bays at once for bigger groups. For full facility buyouts (private events, corporate outings, parties), please email us via the contact page.

Lessons & membership

How do lessons work?
Each coach sets their own price and availability. You book directly with them; they meet you in the bay. Par Out covers the bay and tech; the coach covers the teaching. See the lessons page for more info.
Do I need to be a member to play?
No — anyone can book. Members save $15/hr on every session and get a 30-day advance booking window (public gets 7 days). See memberships for the full breakdown.
Does my whole group get the member rate?
The member rate applies to the bay, but the account holder (you) must be in the bay during the session. Bring whoever — they play at your rate.
