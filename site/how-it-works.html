How it works

Book. Walk in. Swing.

Par Out is self-serve indoor golf, unstaffed on purpose. No check-in desk, no line, no one to wave you in — just a door that knows you're coming. Here's the whole thing, start to finish.

Book a bay

Pick your date, time, and the number of bays you need. Members enjoy a 30-day advance booking window, while the public gets a 7-day window. Pay online, and your spot is secured.

Get your access email

Ten minutes before your start time, we’ll send an email with your digital access link. The link becomes active 10 minutes before your session and stays live until 10 minutes after—giving you plenty of buffer time to walk in and get settled.

Unlock the door

When you arrive, simply open your access email and tap the button to unlock the front door. Head inside and find your numbered bay—the simulator will already be powered up and ready for your booking. Don't forget to share the access email if you're playing with a group!

Log in and pick a course

The TrackMan iO will automatically boot to the player-select screen. Log in with your Trackman account and you're ready to go. Choose from over 250 world-class courses or warm up on the driving range. Fresh Titleist Pro V1s will be waiting for you in the bay.

Play

Track your shot shape, spin, carry, and every metric that matters on-screen after every single swing. We accommodate up to four players per bay. Feel free to bring your own snacks and non-alcoholic drinks.

Walk out

When your time is up, the simulator will automatically power down. Please take a quick moment to tidy up the bay—trash in the bins, and a wipe down of the table helps keep the facility clean. Because the facility is fully automated, please vacate the bay promptly so the next group can start right on time. That’s it!

Running late?

Your door code stays active during your reservation. The clock runs from your booking time, not your walk-in time.

Need to cancel?

Please cancel at least 2 hours before your reservation. We will try to work with you as best as we can.

Got questions?

If something's unclear — booking, door entry, what to bring — email us at support@parout.golf or check the FAQ.
