Privacy Policy

How we handle the information you share with us — in plain language.

Last updated: June 2026  ·  Par Out Golf LLC

This Privacy Policy explains how Par Out Golf LLC ("Par Out," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you visit parout.golf, book a bay, join our email list, or contact us. By using our website, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

We only collect what we need to run the business and stay in touch with you:

  • Information you give us directly. Your email address when you join our list; your name, email, and message details when you submit a private-events inquiry or email us; and booking details (name, contact info, payment) when you reserve a bay.
  • Information collected automatically. When you visit the site, standard analytics data such as pages viewed, approximate location (city/region), device and browser type, and how you arrived at the site. This is collected through cookies and similar technologies.

We do not knowingly collect more than this, and we do not sell your personal information.

2. How we use your information

  • To send you news, offers, and updates you've signed up for.
  • To respond to your inquiries and provide customer support.
  • To process and manage your bookings and payments.
  • To understand how the site is used so we can improve it.
  • To keep the facility and our systems secure, and to comply with the law.

3. The tools and services we use

We rely on a few trusted third-party services to operate. Each only receives the information needed to do its job, and each has its own privacy policy:

  • MailerLite — manages our email list and sends our newsletters and offers.
  • Google Analytics — helps us understand website traffic using cookies. You can opt out with Google's browser add-on.
  • Alba Play — our booking provider; handles bay reservations and payment processing. Payment-card details are handled by Alba Play, not stored by us.
  • FormSubmit — delivers private-events inquiry submissions to our inbox.

4. Cookies

Cookies are small files stored on your device. We use them mainly for analytics (to count visits and understand usage). You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the site will still work, though some features may behave differently.

5. How we share information

We don't sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with the service providers listed above (so they can perform their function), or when required by law, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Par Out, our customers, or others.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Unsubscribe anytime. Every marketing email has an unsubscribe link at the bottom. One click and you're off the list.
  • Access or delete your data. You can ask us what personal information we hold about you and request that we correct or delete it.
  • Opt out of analytics. Use the Google opt-out linked above, or your browser's cookie controls.

Depending on where you live (for example, California or the EU), you may have additional rights over your personal data. To exercise any of these, just email us at the address below and we'll help.

7. Children's privacy

Our website and email list are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

8. Data security & retention

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, and we rely on the security practices of the established providers listed above. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, however, so we can't guarantee absolute security. We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described here or as required by law.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we'll revise the "Last updated" date above. Significant changes may also be announced on the site or by email.
