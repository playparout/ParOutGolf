Par Out Golf — a slice of the great indoors

A 24/7 unstaffed indoor golf simulator facility at 1770 Central Avenue, Colonie, NY 12205 — ten minutes from Albany. Four public Trackman iO bays, up to four players per bay, no bar and no food minimum. Opening winter 2027.

Rates (per bay, per hour, up to 4 players)

  • Peak, Fri–Sun 9am–10pm — $60/hr ($50 members)
  • Off-Peak, Mon–Thu 9am–10pm — $50/hr ($40 members)
  • Night Owl, 10pm–9am every night — $35/hr ($25 members)

Memberships

Early Bird $199/yr, capped at 50 members. Annual $249/yr. Both save $10/hr on every rate and book 30 days in advance; the public books 7 days out.

How access works

Book a bay online, pay, and a door access link is emailed to you. It activates 10 minutes before your session and expires 10 minutes after it ends. Tap the link, the door unlocks, walk to your bay — the simulator is already on. The facility is unstaffed by design; cameras are active throughout.

Good to know

Bring your own clubs — no loaners yet. Food is welcome; non-alcoholic drinks only. Plan about 30 minutes per person for 9 holes, an hour for 18. Independent coaches can rent a bay: coaches@parout.golf.

Contact

Par Out Golf LLC, 1770 Central Avenue, Colonie, NY 12205 · Contact@parout.golf · (518) 727-3442 · open 24 hours, 7 days.

  1. 01

    Book a bay

    Pick your date, time, and how many bays you need. Members book 30 days out; general public gets 7. Pay online and your spot is secured.

  2. 02

    Get your access email

    Ten minutes before your start time we email your digital access link. It goes live 10 minutes prior to your tee time and lasts until 10 minutes after — plenty of buffer to walk in and get settled.

  3. 03

    Unlock the door

    Open the email, tap the button, door opens. Head to your reserved bay — the simulator is already powered up. Forward the email to anyone playing with you.

  4. 04

    Log in and pick a course

    The Trackman iO boots to the player-select screen. Log in with your Trackman account, then choose from over 250 world-class courses or warm up on the range.

  5. 05

    Play

    Shot shape, spin, carry, and every metric that matters, on screen after every swing. Up to four players per bay. Bring your own snacks and non-alcoholic drinks.

  6. 06

    Walk out

    The simulator locks automatically when your session ends, but you're welcome to extend your time if the bay is available. When you're finished, please toss your trash in the bins and give the table a quick wipe so it's ready for the next group. That's it.

The seven people ask first.

Everything else — cancellations, group buyouts, what to do if the sim freezes at 2am — is on the full FAQ page.

How do I get in at 3am?01

Your booking confirmation email contains a door access link. It activates 10 minutes before your session starts and expires 10 minutes after it ends. Tap the link in the email, the door unlocks, walk in.

Is anyone actually there?02

Nope. Par Out is unstaffed by design — that's how we keep the 24/7 hours viable. Cameras are active throughout the facility for everyone's safety.

Do I need to bring my own clubs?03

Yes. At this time we don't have loaner clubs. It's something we're actively looking into.

How many people fit in a bay?04

Up to 4 players on the simulator per session. The lounge area around each bay seats 4, which is fine for a small group hanging out while a foursome plays. Planning something bigger? Reach out and we'll try to work with you.

Can I bring food? What about alcohol?05

Food, yes — order a pizza, pack a cooler, grab takeout on the way. Drinks, non-alcoholic only; we're not licensed to serve or allow alcohol on the premises.

How long should I book for 9 or 18 holes?06

Rough math: about 30 minutes per person for 9 holes, about an hour per person for 18.

Do I need to be a member to play?07

No — anyone can book. Members save $10/hr on every session and get a 30-day advance booking window; the public gets 7 days. Full breakdown here.
